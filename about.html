02. ROBOTICS KNOWLEDGE & TECHNICAL EXPERTISE

Embedded Systems & Firmware

Low-level development on 32-bit ARM Cortex (STM32), ESP32, and AVR/Arduino architectures. Bare-metal peripheral configuration (TIM, PWM, ADC, DMA, USART, I2C, SPI), interrupt-driven event loops, and real-time firmware in C and C++.

Motor Control & Power Electronics

Precision DC motor drive systems utilizing dual H-bridge motor drivers with custom heatsink thermal management, high-current MOSFET switching, active dynamic braking, encoder pulse feedback, and LiPo battery power distribution.

Control Theory & Kinematics

Implementation and real-time parameter tuning of discrete PID (Proportional-Integral-Derivative) controllers for high-velocity line tracking, trajectory correction, differential drive steering kinematics, and dead reckoning.

Sensors & Telemetry

Custom multi-channel optical reflectance sensor arrays (phototransistors/IR emitters) for high-frequency surface contrast sensing, I2C OLED graphical interfaces for onboard live debugging, and IMU sensor fusion.

Mechanical & Hardware Prototyping

CAD design for robotics chassis, laser-cut lightweight acrylic and polycarbonate frames, 3D printed custom motor mounts, brass standoff integration, and clean wiring loom management.

03. HARDWARE SPOTLIGHT (HOMEPAGE BUILD)

The machine pictured on the homepage is a custom high-performance autonomous line racer / mobile robotics platform engineered from the ground up:

  • Chassis: Dual-tier laser-cut clear acrylic chassis with low center of gravity.
  • Processor: 32-bit high-clock microcontroller board with custom pin multiplexing.
  • Drivetrain: Dual high-RPM micro metal gear motors with brass gearboxes and precision output pinions.
  • Driver Stage: Dual discrete H-bridge drivers with finned aluminum heat sinks for thermal endurance under continuous high-frequency PWM switching.
  • Sensor Bar: Front-mounted multi-element wide-angle optical sensor array on a contoured curved substrate for early line deviation detection.
  • Telemetry: Onboard monochrome 0.96" OLED display for real-time sensor threshold calibration and battery monitoring.
  • Power Plant: 2-cell high-discharge LiPo pack secured with quick-release velcro harness.
